Why this school

The prophetic was never meant to be figured out alone.

You've sensed it — the dream that lingered, the word that came true, the pull you couldn't explain. This is a place to understand it, test it, and learn to carry it with humility and order.

I

Rooted in the Word

Every gift is weighed against Scripture. We build on the Bible first, so what you hear can be trusted and tested.

II

Formed in character

Gifting opens doors; character keeps you in the room. We disciple the person, not just the prophetic ability.

III

Sent to serve

The prophetic exists to build up the Church. You'll learn to minister under authority, in community, in love.

The formation

What you'll learn

Six areas of study across the cohort — from the biblical foundations to ministering with wisdom in the local church.

01

Foundations of the Prophetic

The biblical basis, a short history, and how the prophetic gifts function today.

02

Hearing the Voice of God

Discerning God's voice from your own — the ways He speaks and how to grow in listening.

03

Dreams, Visions & Revelation

Receiving, recording, interpreting, and testing what you sense — with sound judgment.

04

Character & the Prophetic Life

Humility, holiness, and accountability — the inner life that sustains a lasting ministry.

05

Ministering Prophetically

Delivery, timing, love, and order — how to steward a word so it builds and never wounds.

06

The Prophet & the Local Church

Serving under authority, honoring leadership, and finding your place in the body.
